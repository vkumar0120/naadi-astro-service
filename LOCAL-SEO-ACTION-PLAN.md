## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Inconsistent NAP:**
   - Don't use "Vaitheeswarankoil" on one site and "Vaitheeswaran Koil" on another
   - Don't vary phone format: Always use +91 95006 39334

2. **Duplicate Content:**
   - Don't copy-paste same content for all city pages
   - Customize travel info, testimonials, and local references

3. **Ignoring Reviews:**
   - Respond to ALL reviews (even 1-star)
   - Thank people personally
   - Never buy fake reviews

4. **No Photos:**
   - Listings without photos get 50% less engagement
   - Upload minimum 10 photos per location

5. **Incomplete Profiles:**
   - Fill every field in Google Business Profile
   - Add business hours, payment methods, attributes
